  • Mort, spectateur et résurrection en Robux avec un ProcessReceipt correct.
  • Décors générés par script : un sous-marin de 1 926 studs et un yacht de 3 852 studs, reconstruits à l'identique.

  • Airlock matchmaking. 8 airlocks (solo to 4 players), countdowns, then a group teleport to a reserved server with team data.
  • Monster AI. Raycast-checked vision cone, hearing, last-known-position search, recalculated pathfinding and respawn out of sight.
  • Animated hiding spots. Wardrobes, lockers, beds and ladders with a peek-through camera and clamped look.
  • Server-authoritative guns. Fire rate, magazine, range and headshots validated on the server.
  • Death, spectate and Robux revive with a correct ProcessReceipt.
  • Script-generated maps: a 1,926-stud submarine and a 3,852-stud yacht, rebuilt identically every time.
